Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026

At CopyCart, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting any information you handle while using our browser extension and web services. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding data collection and usage.

CopyCart is designed with user privacy at its core. We do not collect, store, transmit, or monetize your personal data, browsing history, account credentials, or specific shopping cart contents on external servers. 2. Local Storage and Data

The extension may store custom cart templates and user preferences locally on your device using browser storage APIs (`chrome.storage.local`). This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly export or share a generated link string.
