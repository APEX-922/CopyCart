Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Welcome to CopyCart. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy explains how our browser extension handles your information when you use our service.

2. Data Collection & Local Processing

CopyCart is built with privacy-first principles. Everything runs locally on your machine:

  • We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal shopping history or credentials to any external database.
  • Cart configurations and saved folders are kept securely within your browser's local storage.
  • No external trackers or analytics scripts are embedded within the extension's functional core.

CopyCart interacts with retail platforms (such as Amazon) strictly to parse your active cart items and format shareable URLs.
