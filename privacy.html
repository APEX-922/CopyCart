1. Overview

CopyCart ("the Extension", "we", "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This policy details how data is handled when you use our browser extension to copy, save, and share Amazon shopping carts.

2. Data Collection & Processing

We believe in absolute data minimization. The Extension operates according to the following principles:

  • Active Tab Reading: The Extension accesses open Amazon cart pages solely when you actively trigger the popup. It parses item titles, quantities, and ASIN numbers to build share links or folder lists.
  • Local Storage: All saved cart folders and custom user preferences are stored directly on your computer via chrome.storage.local / browser.storage.local.
  • No Server Collection: We do not host central servers or databases that transmit, analyze, or collect your personal identifiable information (PII).

3. Amazon Associates & Referral Transparency

The Extension generates consolidated cart URLs by formatting standard Amazon links. As an Amazon Associate, the extension author may append a referral tracking tag to generated links. If a user clicks a shared cart link and completes a purchase, a small referral commission may be earned to support extension development. This process involves no personal user profile tracking or data collection.

4. Third-Party Web Interactions

The Extension interacts exclusively with official Amazon domains (e.g., amazon.com, amazon.co.uk, etc.) to perform cart formatting actions. Your browsing activity on Amazon is governed by Amazon's official Privacy Policy.​
