1. Overview

CopyCart ("the Extension", "we", "us") is dedicated to protecting your privacy. This policy details how data is handled when you use our browser extension to copy, save, and share shopping carts.

2. Data Collection & Processing

We believe in absolute data minimization. The Extension operates according to the following principles:

  • Active Tab Reading: The Extension accesses open shopping cart pages solely when you actively trigger the popup. It parses item titles, quantities, and product IDs to build share links or folder lists.
  • Local Storage: All saved cart folders and custom user preferences are stored directly on your computer via chrome.storage.local / browser.storage.local.
  • No Server Collection: We do not host central servers or databases that transmit, analyze, or collect your personal identifiable information (PII).

3. Affiliate & Referral Transparency

The Extension generates consolidated cart URLs by formatting standard checkout links. The extension author may append a referral tracking tag to generated links. If a user clicks a shared cart link and completes a purchase, a small referral commission may be earned to support extension development. This process involves no personal user profile tracking or data collection.

4. Third-Party Web Interactions

The Extension interacts exclusively with supported e-commerce domains to perform cart formatting actions. Your browsing activity on those sites is governed by their respective official privacy policies.

5. Changes & Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect enhancements or changes in browser permissions. Any updates will be reflected on this page with a revised effective date.

6. Support & Contact

If you have questions or concerns regarding this policy or the extension's data handling practices, please contact us via our official repository support tab or Web Store listing page.
